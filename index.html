Public filings · original sources

See what changed, then read the evidence.

StockFilingChanges compares selected passages in consecutive annual 10-K filings and filing-matched SEC XBRL facts. It helps readers locate changes; it does not judge whether they are good or bad.

Built for careful reading

  • View added, removed, and revised Item 1A risk-factor passages side by side.
  • Compare selected reported financial facts from the corresponding SEC filings.
  • Open both original filing documents to check context.
  • Save a local watchlist; no account required.

Some companies or sections may not be comparable. The app identifies unavailable comparisons rather than inventing an answer. Automated text matching can still include formatting changes, so original filings remain authoritative.
