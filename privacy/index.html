Privacy Policy

Effective October 8, 2026

StockFilingChanges does not require an account. The app stores your saved ticker watchlist on your device. We do not operate a user-profile database, and the app does not include advertising or third-party analytics SDKs.

Network requests

The app retrieves public filing-comparison JSON from our GitHub Pages site. GitHub may receive standard web-request information such as your IP address and request time under GitHub’s privacy statement. If you tap an original filing link, your browser opens the SEC website, subject to the SEC’s own privacy practices. We do not attach your watchlist to these requests.

Subscriptions

Apple processes in-app purchases. The app checks Apple StoreKit entitlements to unlock Premium; we do not receive your payment-card details. Apple may provide sales and subscription reports to the developer through App Store Connect under Apple’s terms.

Support email

If you email us for support, we receive your email address and the information you choose to send. We use it only to respond and resolve the request. Do not email brokerage credentials or sensitive financial information. You may request deletion of a support correspondence by emailing ixxzhu@gmail.com.

Your choices

Remove watchlist entries in the app or uninstall it to delete its locally stored watchlist. Manage or cancel a subscription in your Apple Account settings. You can use the free catalog without subscribing.
