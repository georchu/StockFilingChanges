Support

Questions about a filing comparison or a subscription? Email ixxzhu@gmail.com.

Filing data looks wrong

Send the company ticker, the two filing dates, and the relevant SEC source link. Automated comparison can confuse formatting changes with substantive wording changes; we investigate documented mismatches and correct or remove them.

Premium did not unlock

In the app, open About → Restore Purchases. Make sure your device uses the same Apple Account that bought the subscription. If the problem remains, email us with the app version and an Apple purchase reference; do not send payment-card details.

Cancel or request a refund

Manage the subscription in your device’s Apple Account subscription settings. Apple handles App Store refunds at reportaproblem.apple.com.
